```md
# Cali Personal Injury Lawyer — Project Context

## 1. Project Overview

Cali Personal Injury Lawyer is a modern personal injury law firm website concept for:

**calipersonalinjurylawyer.com**

The project is intentionally open-ended.

No existing website content, visual identity, copy, design system, brand guidelines, or assets have been provided.

The purpose is to demonstrate how far a modern AI-assisted web development workflow can take a legal website when given creative freedom.

---

# 2. Project Goal

Create a premium legal website that feels modern, sophisticated, human, and trustworthy.

The website should demonstrate:

- Strong visual design
- Excellent UX
- Modern frontend architecture
- Responsive design
- Sophisticated animation
- Strong typography
- Creative content
- Conversion-focused UX
- Technical SEO
- Performance awareness
- Accessibility

---

# 3. Target Audience

Primary audience:

People in California who may need legal assistance after an injury or accident.

Potential visitors may include people dealing with:

- Car accidents
- Truck accidents
- Motorcycle accidents
- Pedestrian accidents
- Workplace injuries
- Catastrophic injuries
- Wrongful death
- Other personal injury situations

The audience may arrive under stressful circumstances.

Therefore the website must communicate:

- Calm
- Confidence
- Clarity
- Empathy
- Competence
- Immediate next steps

---

# 4. User Psychology

A visitor may be asking:

- Can this firm help me?
- Do they understand my situation?
- Can I trust them?
- What should I do next?
- Will contacting them cost anything?
- Does my situation qualify?
- How difficult will this process be?

The website should answer these questions progressively.

Do not overwhelm the user with legal terminology.

---

# 5. Brand Personality

The brand should feel:

- Confident
- Human
- Calm
- Experienced
- Direct
- Protective
- Sophisticated
- Modern

Avoid:

- Aggressive sales language
- Fear-based marketing
- Excessive legal jargon
- Cheap promotional language
- Overused clichés

---

# 6. Visual Concept

Primary visual concept:

**Modern Legal Editorial**

Supporting visual concepts:

**Glassmorphism**

**3D Abstract Minimalism**

**Architectural Composition**

**Premium Photography**

The design should use depth and layering to communicate protection and structure.

---

# 7. Visual Metaphors

Potential visual metaphors include:

- Protection
- Structure
- Balance
- Strength
- Recovery
- Movement
- Clarity
- Guidance
- Stability

These metaphors should remain sophisticated and abstract.

Avoid overly literal legal imagery.

Do not rely heavily on:

- Gavel photographs
- Scales of justice icons
- Courthouse stock images
- Generic lawyer stock photography

---

# 8. Homepage Philosophy

The homepage should tell a story.

Possible narrative:

1. Something changed.
2. You need clarity.
3. You need someone in your corner.
4. Your case needs to be understood.
5. Your legal team builds the case.
6. The firm fights for the client's interests.
7. The visitor is invited to take the next step.

---

# 9. Core UX Concept

A key interactive element should help visitors identify their situation.

Example:

**What happened?**

Options:

- Car Accident
- Truck Accident
- Motorcycle Accident
- Pedestrian Accident
- Workplace Injury
- Catastrophic Injury
- Wrongful Death
- Other

This should feel like an elegant guided experience rather than a conventional form.

---

# 10. Content Freedom

The project has no supplied content.

Claude may create original marketing copy.

However, all real-world legal claims must remain replaceable and must not be presented as verified facts unless provided by the user.

Demo content should be structured so that real firm information can later replace it.

---

# 11. Business Objective

Primary objective:

Generate consultation/contact opportunities.

Secondary objectives:

- Build trust
- Explain services
- Establish authority
- Educate visitors
- Make the firm memorable
- Provide clear next steps

---

# 12. Technical Objective

Build a scalable Next.js website that can later be connected to real:

- Firm information
- Attorney profiles
- Practice areas
- Testimonials
- Case results
- Contact systems
- CRM
- Analytics
- CMS

without requiring a complete frontend rewrite.

---

# 13. Development Constraint

The project must be completed in exactly four phases.

No additional development phase should be introduced.

---

# 14. Success Definition

The project is successful when a visitor sees it and thinks:

"This does not look like another generic lawyer website."

At the same time:

"It still feels trustworthy enough that I would consider contacting this firm."
```
