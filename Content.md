# Cali Personal Injury Lawyer — Content

## Content Status

This project currently has no official client-provided content.

All content created during development is conceptual/demo content unless explicitly marked as verified.

---

# CONTENT PRINCIPLES

Content should be:

- Human
- Clear
- Confident
- Concise
- Empathetic
- Conversion-focused
- Easy to scan

Avoid:

- Excessive legal jargon
- Generic filler
- Repetitive statements
- Fake credentials
- Fake testimonials
- Fake case results
- Unsupported legal guarantees

---

# BRAND MESSAGE

The central brand idea is:

**When life changes in an instant, the right legal guidance can help you understand what comes next.**

This idea may evolve during development.

---

# CORE MESSAGE THEMES

## Protection

Clients need someone who understands the situation and protects their interests.

## Clarity

Legal processes can feel confusing. The website should make the next step understandable.

## Advocacy

The firm should be presented as an advocate for people navigating difficult circumstances.

## Human Support

The website should recognize that personal injury is about people, not just cases.

---

# PROCESS

Potential process:

## 01 — Listen

Understand what happened and what you are facing.

## 02 — Investigate

Build a clear picture of the facts surrounding the case.

## 03 — Build

Develop a strong case strategy based on the available evidence.

## 04 — Advocate

Pursue the client's interests with determination.

## 05 — Move Forward

Help the client understand the next stage of the legal process.
