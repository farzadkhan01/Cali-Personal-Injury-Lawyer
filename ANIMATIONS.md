````md
# Cali Personal Injury Lawyer — Animation System

## Philosophy

Animation should create:

- Depth
- Continuity
- Focus
- Emotion
- Feedback

Animation should never exist simply because it is possible.

---

# 1. PAGE LOAD

Use subtle staged entrance.

Possible sequence:

1. Background
2. Navigation
3. Hero label
4. Hero headline
5. Hero copy
6. CTA
7. Visual artwork
8. Floating details

Avoid long loading sequences.

---

# 2. TEXT REVEAL

Large headlines may use:

- Fade
- Vertical reveal
- Mask reveal
- Subtle blur-to-sharp

Avoid excessive letter-by-letter animations.

---

# 3. SCROLL REVEAL

Sections can reveal as they enter the viewport.

Use:

- Opacity
- Translate
- Scale

Keep movement subtle.

---

# 4. GLASS INTERACTIONS

Glass cards may respond to hover with:

- Slight elevation
- Small translation
- Border highlight
- Subtle glow

Do not make cards aggressively move.

---

# 5. 3D ARTWORK

3D artwork may have subtle:

- Floating motion
- Parallax
- Rotation
- Light movement

Avoid continuous distracting animation.

---

# 6. BUTTONS

Buttons may include:

- Arrow movement
- Background transition
- Scale
- Icon translation

Keep interaction quick.

---

# 7. NAVIGATION

Navigation may transition smoothly when:

- Opening
- Closing
- Scrolling
- Changing background state

---

# 8. PAGE TRANSITIONS

If implemented, transitions should be short and unobtrusive.

Do not make navigation feel slow.

---

# 9. REDUCED MOTION

Respect:

```css
prefers-reduced-motion
```
````
